# Other lines in Bruce's sessions

Short notes on lines outside sports grounds; each becomes its own file when it is run again.

## US parking lots (line striping)
- Customer: "large groups + line-striping contractors"; outdoor is a leaning, not a rule (parking
  structure operators were accepted and scaled).
- The line that wrote the source test as a hard rule (thread 01a05840): each source tried on 30
  accounts - the parking operators' association 26.67% and 30% strict accounts, scaled into
  rolling 25-account packages; a second association 9.8% -> 17.65% after one improvement; the
  state DOT prequalification list 3.33%, stopped; a generic directory crawl 0 of 40.
- Roles tested by function, not title: approves budget or suppliers, evaluates the technology,
  owns the work or equipment, controls purchasing, or can take a demo.
- Report three numbers apart: pool size, deduplicated public routes, strict named role addresses.
- 902 pages -> 510 routes; 36 A with a name. The dedupe rule changed three times.

## US agriculture and buying committees
- Customer: contractors who own or schedule excavators for drainage and land improvement (an
  agent proposal he accepted, not his own wording); farms not in the first wave.
- The pool is capped ("mother_pool_v1.93; no more expansion after it"); then all capacity goes to
  depth. Acceptance is verified direct work addresses, not pool size.
- Equipment-dealer lists on 30 accounts: 6.67% (should have been "improve once", was stopped by
  mistake) and 3.33% (stop). A lane with two waves under 10% direct and 15% A/B/C -> hold.
- Strict routes per wave ran at about 5-15% of accounts; coverage of A/B/C 8% in large waves -
  the missing piece was a second verified buying role, not the address (direct addresses were
  found for 80-100%).
- 1,888 organisations -> 82 routes (1.75%); of 17,065 qualified addresses, C 1.74%, D 98.26%:
  without role binding almost everything is a general inbox.
- A pool of 128,685 organisations with no yield check produced nothing sendable; the user
  stopped it.

## From his own instructions (supplement)

- A public mailbox on the organisation's own domain is a route - it can forward; a non-work or
  personal mailbox must not enter the pool. Judge the local part, not the domain.
- Small organisations: keep one route and hand it over; do not spend the batch on all of them.
- Use the pool version as it stands, then re-check the source for updates after the research
  rather than waiting for a frozen copy.
- Report coverage completeness next to quality: "all quality checks green" does not mean the pool
  is complete. Blockers must be 100% resolved; key gaps may ship but must be stated.
- A new line is measured on 30 accounts before it is scaled: improve once, or stop.
- Keep golf out of the non-golf lines by name, not by inference: the line is called 非高尔夫 for
  a reason.
