We are Damen

Damen is an international shipyard group but at its heart, there is still a family company. We operate in every market where we see an opportunity to improve, innovate or invest. At Damen we make time to listen to our clients. Our open, collaborative approach has been a key driver of our product portfolio’s evolution. Exceeding your expectations is our most important goal; after all, it’s your future we’re building.
