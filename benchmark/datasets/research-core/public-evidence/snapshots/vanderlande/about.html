About Vanderlande

Vanderlande is the long-term partner of choice for the world’s leading airports and parcel operators — delivering automation solutions, operational software, and lifecycle services that drive performance, reliability, and sustainable growth.

Our company profile

Meet the Board

Our Management Board consists of six members who are responsible for setting Vanderlande’s strategy, objectives and policies, and for overseeing the company’s overall direction and governance. Drawing on their combined leadership experience and international expertise, they guide the long-term development of the business and support its sustainable growth and continued success.

Toyota Automated Logistics Group

The Toyota Automated Logistics Group is a global partner for integrated logistic process automation. As part of Toyota Industries Corporation (TICO), the group now brings together: Toyota L&F, Toyota Automated Logistics (TAL) and Vanderlande’s Airport and Distribution business. TAL unites the expertise of Bastian Solutions, Vanderlande’s Warehousing business and viastore under one brand, strengthening the group’s ability to deliver scalable systems, intelligent software and life‑cycle services across industries helping customers meet the challenges specific to their industries.

History

The origins of Vanderlande lie in the Dutch town of Veghel, where Eddie van der Lande established the company in 1949. His family initially refurbished and later produced machines for the textile industry, before moving into the manufacture of hoisting apparatus, cranes and conveyor belts for bulky materials and barrels of oil.

Facts and Figures

Our market-leading baggage handling systems are capable of moving over 4 billion pieces of baggage around the world per year. These are active in more than 600 airports, including 17 of the world’s top 20. In addition, over 440 of Vanderlande’s security lanes are installed at checkpoints worldwide. More than 52 million parcels are sorted by our systems every day, which have been installed for the world’s leading parcel handling companies.

Innovation

Operating internationally means strong competition. Continuous innovation is the key to winning in a world that is changing faster than ever, which is why it is essential for Vanderlande to develop cutting-edge solutions.
