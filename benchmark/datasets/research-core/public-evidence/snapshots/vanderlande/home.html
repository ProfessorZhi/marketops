What we do

Vanderlande is a market-leading, global partner for future-proof logistic process automation in the airports and parcel sectors. Its extensive portfolio of integrated solutions – innovative systems, intelligent software and life-cycle services – results in the realisation of fast, reliable and efficient automation technology.

Airports

Vanderlande can meet any airport’s demands in full – from bag drop and passenger security checks, through to onward conveyance, storage, sortation, flight make-up and baggage reclaim.

Parcel

Parcel handling companies expect the most cost-effective systems, with the highest available performance. Vanderlande understands what it takes to operate a successful depot.
