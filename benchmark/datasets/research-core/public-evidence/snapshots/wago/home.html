WAGO Cybersecurity Collector

The WAGO Cybersecurity Collector monitors segmented network areas and securely transmits collected data to a central system, without burdening the network. This data is then available to systems such as SOC, SIEM, firewalls, or OT asset management platforms.

7/17/2026

Björn Twiehaus Appointed New CEO of the WAGO Group



The WAGO Group is preparing for its next chapter of growth: on September 15, Björn Twiehaus (48) will take over the position of Chief Executive Officer (CEO) of the family-owned company headquartered in Minden. He succeeds Jürgen Koopsingraven, who currently serves as interim CEO in addition to his role as Chief Financial Officer (CFO).

Since pioneering spring pressure connection technology, WAGO has grown into one of the world’s leading providers of electrical interconnection and automation technology. With the appointment of Björn Twiehaus, WAGO will continue this success story and further drive international growth, innovation, and business development.
