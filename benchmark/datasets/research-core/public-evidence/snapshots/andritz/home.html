CREATING GROWTH THAT MATTERS

ANDRITZ creates growth that matters — for the business, the people and our planet. With a passion for innovative technologies, we drive the green transition by helping industries worldwide enhance efficiency, reduce waste and minimize emissions. We help our customers achieve their sustainability and business goals, whether driving decarbonization, accelerating digitalization or strengthening customer service.
 

ANDRITZ GROUP

  • International technology group headquartered in Austria, with around 30,000 employees and over 280 locations in more than 80 countries.
  • Supplies plants, equipment, automation, and services for pulp and paper, metals, hydropower, and environment & energy sectors.
  • Founded in 1852, ANDRITZ has grown into a global leader known for engineering excellence and long-term customer partnerships.
  • A balanced market presence globally and diversified customer base.
  • Purpose-driven to make a lasting impact by enabling sustainable solutions for the industries of the future.
     

OUR BUSINESS AREAS

  • Pulp & Paper: Technologies and services for producing pulp, paper, tissue, and board — including automation, recycling, and decarbonization solutions.
  • Metals: Equipment and turnkey lines for metals processing and forming, serving industries like automotive, steel, and battery manufacturing.
  • Hydropower: Electromechanical systems, services, and digital solutions for new and existing hydropower plants supporting renewable energy generation.
  • Environment & Energy: Sustainable technologies such as carbon capture, P2X and green hydrogen, separation, recycling, and pumps for various industrial sectors.
     

FINANCIAL HIGHLIGHTS AND SHARE

  • Revenue of €7.9 billion, EBITA of €648 million, and order intake of €8.9 billion in 2025.
