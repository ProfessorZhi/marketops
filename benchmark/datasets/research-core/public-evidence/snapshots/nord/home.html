NORD at SPS 2026: Focus on digital services

2026-11-09

From 24 to 26 November, NORD DRIVESYSTEMS will present its current portfolio at SPS 2026 in Nuremberg, and will focus on the digital services the system provider has to offer to its customers. A practical model on the subject of digital twins illustrates the relation between virtual commissioning and real-life drive optimisation. Offers such as the IIoT solution NORD DRIVE MONITOR and the NORD ECO service complement the trade fair presentation.
