Engineering with purpose

GEA is one of the world’s largest systems suppliers for the food, beverage and pharmaceutical sectors. Our portfolio includes machinery and plants as well as advanced process technology, components and comprehensive services. Guided by a strong sense of purpose, our more than 18,000 dedicated employees work to enhance the sustainability and efficiency of production processes globally.

Engineering for a better world

We engineer resource-efficient solutions for a better world and to support the sustainability goals of our customers. And we promote the highest environmental and social standards at our own sites and in procurement. We aim to become the most attractive employer in the engineering industry with greater diversity, equal opportunity and integration, and by offering long-term career development prospects. To make these ambitions concrete, we have defined measurable ESG targets as part of GEA’s Mission 30 strategy.

Leadership change at GEA: Klaus Stojentin to succeed Stefan Klebert as CEO

The Supervisory Board of GEA Group AG today resolved on a leadership change at the helm of the company as well as related changes in the composition of the Executive Board. Stefan Klebert (61), CEO of GEA, will step down from his position at his own request and by mutual agreement with the Supervisory Board following the conclusion of the 2027 Annual General Meeting on April 28, 2027.

GEA delivers strong third quarter: order intake rises substantially, revenue and profitability further improved

GEA continued its positive business performance in the third quarter of 2026. Order intake developed particularly strongly, growing organically by 19.5 percent. This was driven by both a strong base business and large orders totaling EUR 294.8 million. GEA also further increased revenue and profitability. The Group confirmed its outlook for fiscal year 2026, which had been raised in July.

GEA receives Top Employer seal for Asia-Pacific and is now certified in 21 countries

GEA is now certified as a Top Employer in 21 countries across four continents. With eleven new certifications in the Asia-Pacific region, the company meets the requirements for the APAC Regional Seal of the Top Employers Institute for the first time – alongside the European Regional Seal GEA already holds.
